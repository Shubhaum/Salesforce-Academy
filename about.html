About Shashi Technologies

Learn. Build. Get Hired.

Shashi Technologies was founded with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world Salesforce careers. Unlike traditional training institutes, our mentors are active professionals working on enterprise Salesforce implementations in leading global organizations.

Our Mission

To create industry-ready Salesforce professionals by providing practical training, real project exposure, career mentorship and placement assistance. We believe students deserve more than recorded videos and theoretical concepts. They deserve guidance from professionals who are currently solving real business challenges.

Why Students Choose Us

Career Focused Learning
