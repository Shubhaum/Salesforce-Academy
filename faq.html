Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining.

Do I need coding experience?

No. Beginners can start with Salesforce Administration and gradually move toward development.

Can final-year students join?

Absolutely. Our programs are designed for students looking for internships and placements.

Do you provide internship certificates?

Yes. Certificates are provided upon successful completion of internship requirements.

Are classes online or offline?

Both. We offer hybrid learning options.

Will I work on projects?

Yes. Real-world Salesforce projects are part of our learning process.

Do you guarantee placements?

We provide placement assistance, mentorship, interview preparation and referrals where possible. Hiring decisions are made by employers.
